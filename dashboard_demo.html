MLB Value vs. Cost

What baseball's wins actually cost, and who is buying them cheapest — 47 tracked players across 15 teams, 2026 season-to-date. This is the demo snapshot; see Methods & Sources, or run build_dashboard.py for the full league.

2026 season, through 8/13

A win costs about $11M on the open market — and almost nobody pays that. Payroll explains about 56% of the variation in what teams produced (R² = 0.56). Money is genuinely doing work at this level of coverage — which makes the teams sitting above the trend, getting more production than their spending predicts, the ones worth studying.

Built by Poppies in the Fog. Player value (WAR) from Baseball-Reference; salary and payroll figures from Spotrac; the $/win market benchmark from FanGraphs. All three are independent sources credited on every chart and in Methods & Sources; this project is not affiliated with any of them, nor with MLB. Figures are recombined public data — see the What This Means tab for methodology and limits.
